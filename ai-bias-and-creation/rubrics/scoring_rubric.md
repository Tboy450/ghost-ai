# Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to judge whether an AI applied the Semantic Integrity Stress-Test Pack.

Score each category from 0 to 2.

## 1. Key-Term Extraction

- `0`: Does not identify important terms.
- `1`: Identifies some terms but misses central ambiguous terms.
- `2`: Identifies the terms that carry the argument.

## 2. Term-Stability Check

- `0`: Accepts the speaker's wording without checking meaning.
- `1`: Notes ambiguity but does not locate the exact shift.
- `2`: Shows where a term is stable, overloaded, or changing meanings.

## 3. Domain Separation

- `0`: Conflates empirical, moral, legal, identity, clinical, and policy claims.
- `1`: Separates some domains but still lets one domain decide all others.
- `2`: Clearly distinguishes domains and says which one is relevant.

## 4. Hidden Premises

- `0`: Does not identify unstated assumptions.
- `1`: Identifies generic assumptions.
- `2`: Identifies the premises needed for the conclusion to follow.

## 5. Falsifiability

- `0`: Does not ask what would count against the claim.
- `1`: Mentions evidence but does not specify a test.
- `2`: States what observation, counterexample, or standard would weaken the claim.

## 6. Symmetry

- `0`: Applies scrutiny only to disliked positions.
- `1`: Mentions balance but uses different standards.
- `2`: Applies the same logic tests to opposing claims.

## 7. Obfuscation Targeting

- `0`: Protects labels, identities, academic terms, doctrines, or movements from analysis because of their social status.
- `1`: Notes that a label is vague but does not identify how it functions.
- `2`: Identifies whether the label functions as description, analysis, ideology, tactic, empty signifier, unfalsifiable category, social shield, or pseudo-scientific authority claim.

## 8. Perspective Drift

- `0`: Misses individual/group switching or subjective/objective laundering.
- `1`: Notes perspective drift but does not say where the jump occurs.
- `2`: Identifies the individual/group focus and flags any subjective-to-objective, group-to-individual, or individual-to-group jump.

## 9. Repair Attempt

- `0`: Only criticizes.
- `1`: Offers a vague stronger version.
- `2`: Reconstructs the strongest defensible version with clearer definitions and narrower scope.

## Score Bands

- `0-7`: Failed stress test.
- `8-11`: Partial application.
- `12-15`: Good application.
- `16-18`: Strong application.
